# 2026-08-06 Canonization Review

CAP closes the bounded composition gap left intentionally open by the six independent artifacts.

Accepted design decisions:

1. CAP owns a new composite result, not the native source verdicts.
2. Six normative surfaces have exclusive domains.
3. Source statuses are opaque and carried with owner and meaning URI.
4. Domain mapping is explicit and reviewable.
5. Result derivation is deterministic and fail closed.
6. Tool failure, blocking conflict, decisive negative, unknown, partial coverage and full coverage have fixed precedence.
7. Numeric averaging, majority vote and silent substitution are forbidden.
8. Exact source revisions are asymmetric historical pins.
9. A bounded acceptable record is not a global certification or world-truth claim.
